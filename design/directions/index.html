The Player: three directions

The notation is the app's real alphaTab engraving of Song 2 at 20 px between staff lines, with the real sticking. What all three share

  • Staff lines 20 px apart; glance numbers (section, bar, pass, tempo) at 64 px or more; section names and button labels at 32 px or more; every mid-song target at least 64 px.
  • Section names are drawn by the page, in a lane above a line that opens a section, so they never cover a note. Today they sit on top of the first beat.
  • The song map names short sections such as a one-bar "into chorus" outside the strip, so every fill is labelled.
  • Library and settings are two small icons in a corner. They are used between songs, not from the throne.
