Lamp: spec sheet
One face, Atkinson Hyperlegible Next, made for telling letters apart at a distance. Sizes are CSS px at 2194 × 1100 (31.5" 4K at 175%), read from 2 m, where 1 px ≈ 0.32 mm.

Numbers use tabular figures. A section name longer than its space (chorus to break, 880 px at 120) steps down to 88, then 64, never below.
