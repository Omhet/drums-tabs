# Sonne - Rammstein
# read from the drum-score video https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1A00CVLGM7Q (channel 알려드럼)
# 4/4, quarter = 75 (marked on the first line), 75 bars, no repeat signs
#
# Grid: sixteen columns a bar, 1 e & a 2 e & a 3 e & a 4 e & a.
#   x  cymbal hit (CR, RD, closed HH)     o  drum hit, or open hi-hat on the HH row
#   O  accented drum hit                  f  flam (grace note before the hit)
#   .  nothing
# A "<" after a pattern name in the bar list means a crescendo hairpin over that bar.
#
# Key for this channel (confirmed by the user 2026-11-22): triangle above the staff
# = ride, circled x = crash, x above the staff = hi-hat with "o" over it = open,
# top space = high tom, second line = mid tom, third space = snare,
# second space = floor tom, bottom space = kick.
#
# This file is the source of tab.mid: edit it and run the score-video-tab
# skill's to_midi.py with --first-bar N --write (score bar N = song bar 1).
#
# Almost every hi-hat note in this score has the "0" over it, so the groove is
# an open hi-hat throughout; the few closed ones are read as printed.
#
# Where it departs from the score:
#  - Double kicks. The score writes a 32nd-note pair of kicks on the "a" of a
#    beat in G4, G5, F4 and F8 (bars 16, 34, 31, 59). The grid holds sixteenths,
#    so only the first of the pair is here; the second, a 32nd later, is not.
#  - The last crash (bar 75) has a choke mark (a fist) over it. The grid has no
#    choke, so it is a plain crash.

  BD  o . . o . o . . o . . o . o . .
