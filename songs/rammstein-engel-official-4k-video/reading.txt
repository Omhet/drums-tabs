# Engel - Rammstein
# read from the drum-score video https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BrhjyBHtkio (channel 알려드럼)
# 4/4, quarter = 96 (marked on the first line), 105 bars, no repeat signs
#
# Grid: sixteen columns a bar, 1 e & a 2 e & a 3 e & a 4 e & a.
#   x  cymbal hit (CR, RD, closed HH)     o  drum hit, or open hi-hat on the HH row
#   O  accented drum hit                  f  flam (grace note before the hit)
#   d  double: the hit and another a 32nd after it
#   .  nothing
# A "<" after a pattern name in the bar list means a crescendo hairpin over that bar.
# A "!" after one means that bar was read apart from the bars that look like it,
# on purpose (see below).
#
# Key for this channel (confirmed by the user 2026-11-22): triangle above the staff
# = ride, circled x = crash, x above the staff = hi-hat with "o" over it = open,
# top space = high tom, second line = mid tom, third space = snare,
# second space = floor tom, bottom space = kick.
#
# This file is the source of tab.mid: edit it and run the score-video-tab
# skill's to_midi.py with --first-bar N --write (score bar N = song bar 1).
